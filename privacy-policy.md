# Notes privacy policy

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

Notes is a general-purpose notepad published by the developer at
**klixup@gmail.com**. The app does not require an account.

## Notes and device storage

Notes, folders, checklists, note-lock hashes and voice recordings are stored on
your device. Note text and recordings are not sent to advertising services.
Local app data remains on the device until you delete it or clear Notes storage
in Android settings.

## Microphone and voice notes

The microphone is used only when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings
are stored on your device and are not included in JSON backups. You can delete
recordings from the note or Attachments screen.

## Advertising and consent

Notes uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may process device identifiers
(including the advertising ID), IP address or general location, ad interactions,
diagnostics and performance data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and,
where permitted, personalize ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform collects
required consent choices. Where available, you can change them in **Settings →
Ad privacy choices**. Ad removal pauses ads while the entitlement is active. An
optional rewarded video may grant 30 ad-free minutes.

See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[information about Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Purchases

Google Play processes payment details, renewals and refunds. Notes receives the
product and purchase status, plus purchase tokens needed to confirm or restore
an entitlement. Notes does not receive your card number. If ownership cannot be
checked, ads are held until it can be determined.

## Backups and sharing

You create exports and backups only when you request them, then choose where to
share them. JSON backups contain notes, folders and checklists, but not voice
recordings. Backup files contain your note content, so store them somewhere
private. Offline summaries and math helpers run on your device.

## Deletion and retention

You can delete notes and recordings in Notes. Deleted notes remain in Recently
Deleted for up to 30 days, where you can restore them or permanently remove
them. You can clear all local Notes data through Android settings. Google
handles advertising data according to its policies; Google Play handles
purchase records.

## Children

Notes is a general-purpose app and is not directed to children under 13.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact **klixup@gmail.com**.
